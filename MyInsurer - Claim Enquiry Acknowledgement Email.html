About this service

We are the MyInsurer claims team, appointed by your insurer to assess and handle your claim on its behalf.

This is a home insurance claims service, not a home emergency call-out service. Reporting your claim does not book an emergency contractor visit. We may need further information, reports or an inspection before we can make a decision on cover.

Our attached guide, Making a Claim, explains how the process works.

Preparing for our call

If relevant and available, please have these ready:

✓  Photographs of the loss or damage, where safe to take.

✓  Receipts or other evidence of ownership for lost, damaged or stolen items.

✓  Any repair quotes or reports you already have.

✓  Relevant reference numbers or correspondence, such as a crime reference number or a letter from someone claiming against you.

We will explain what information we need for your particular claim when we speak.

In the meantime

•  Please take reasonable steps to prevent further loss or damage.

•  If you arrange urgent temporary repairs for that purpose, keep photographs and itemised receipts.

•  Keep damaged items where safe and practical. If disposal is necessary for safety or hygiene reasons, take photographs first where possible.

•  Please speak to us before authorising permanent repairs, unless urgent work is necessary to prevent further loss or damage.

If someone is making a claim against you, please pass their correspondence to us and speak to us before admitting liability or agreeing any payment.

If there is anything we should take into account when communicating with you or handling your claim, please let us know. This could include communication needs, health issues, bereavement or anything else affecting how we support you.
