What happens next

One of our claims team will call you within the next one to two working days to talk through your claim and agree the next steps. After that call, we will set you up with your own secure online claim page, where you can track progress, upload your photos and documents, and message us at any time.

What to have ready

Having these ready will help when we speak, and will move your claim along quickly:

✓  Photographs of all the damage

✓  Receipts, invoices or proof of purchase for damaged or stolen items

✓  Any repair quotes you can obtain

✓  Anything specific to this claim, for example a crime reference number or a plumber's report

In the meantime

A few things that will help your claim:

•  Keep any damaged items. Please do not throw anything away until we advise you

•  If emergency repairs are needed to prevent further damage, go ahead, but keep all receipts

•  Please do not authorise any permanent repairs until we have assessed the claim

Please note: this email confirms we have your claim. It is not a decision on your claim or an acceptance of liability. We will assess your claim against the terms and conditions of your policy, and your cover and any exclusions are set out in your policy documents. Your handler will explain anything specific to your claim, and asking for information is a normal part of assessing a claim and never means a decision has been made.
